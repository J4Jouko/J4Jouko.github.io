A LITTLE ABOUT ME

3D Artist · Character & Creature Art · Game Industry

Hey, I'm Juliette!

Thanks a lot for stopping by and taking the time to explore my work ^^

I'm a French-Greek 3D Artist specializing in characters and creatures, and I'm currently looking for new opportunities in the game industry.

I love creating characters piece by piece until they start to feel alive~ From shaping their silhouette and personality in 3D to bringing them into the game. I particularly enjoy the balance between artistic expression and technical problem-solving that comes with 3D art.

Over the years, I've worked on a variety of game projects, including adventure games, boss fights, couch games and VR experiences, using both Unity and Unreal Engine. I'm comfortable working across the 3D pipeline, from modeling and sculpting to retopology, texturing and in-game integration.

I've also gained 2 years of professional experience at Thales, working as 2D/3D Artist & Graphic/Game Designer on serious games, simulations and interactive learning experiences. I worked on 3D environments and props, asset integration in Unity, art direction, and contributed to game design and the creation of interactive educational experiences.

Before Thales, I worked at the video game startup Sporty Peppers, where I was responsible for a wide range of artistic tasks, including 3D modeling, texturing and integration, 2D graphic assets, UI, marketing visuals, rigging and animation.

Before choosing game art, I studied Geology. Eventually, I decided to follow what had always been there: my passion for drawing, creating worlds and making games.

Maybe we'll get to create something together!
