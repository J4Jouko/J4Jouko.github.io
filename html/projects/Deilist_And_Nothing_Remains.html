Game Overview

Before time itself, the Celestians ruled over their own worlds until the birth of End. Playing as End, a newborn Celestian and the embodiment of Doom, the player must defeat seven powerful Celestians and bring an end to their era.

Deilist: And Nothing Remains is a fast-paced Boss Rush / Third-Person Shooter focused on intense one-on-one encounters. Each boss has its own arena, visual identity, attack patterns and gameplay challenges.

For this project, our team developed a playable encounter against Celaena, the Celestian of Observation, inspired by the harpy Celaeno from Greek mythology.

Art Direction

Each Celestian was designed around a specific concept and environment.

For Celaena, the visual direction combines bird of prey imagery with Greco-Roman architecture, resulting in a monumental birdcage-like arena.

Her design reflects her personality: proud and stern on the surface, but driven by fear of End and the destruction it represents.

For the final look, we focused on a semi-realistic and stylized approach, using cool blue tones contrasted with gold accents to reinforce her celestial status.

Celaena - From Concept to 3D

Concept Art by Floriane and 3D Model by Juliette.

I was responsible for translating our concept artist's design into a fully functional 3D character.

The goal was to stay as close as possible to the original concept while adapting the design to the constraints of a real-time game character.

One of the main challenges was her three pairs of wings. Their geometry had to be carefully designed and optimized to prevent intersections between the wings during animation while maintaining the silhouette and visual impact of the original concept.

Character Breakdown - Front

  • High Poly: ZBrush sculpt focused on the main shapes, proportions and ornamental details.
  • Low Poly: Retopology and optimization in ZBrush and Blender, with a focus on keeping the silhouette and deformation areas clean.
  • Texturing: PBR texturing created in Substance 3D Painter.

Gold accents were used for the mask and accessories, contrasted with cool blue tones across the rest of the character.

Character Breakdown - Back

The back view highlights the wing structure and the optimization choices made to keep the three pairs of wings visually distinct while allowing enough clearance for animation.

Environment Texturing

Celaena's Arena

While the environmental assets were modeled by other members of the team, I was responsible for their full PBR texturing.

My goal was to make the different assets feel like part of the same environment while following the original concepts.

The arena uses a shared light marble material, with subtle blue undertones and small touches of pale pink. Blue and violet hues were reinforced in some metallic elements to complement the lighting and create stronger visual harmony.

To reproduce the ornamental patterns from the concepts, I created and used custom alpha masks in Substance 3D Painter, allowing detailed decorations without unnecessarily increasing the geometry.

Post-Mortem

What I Learned

  • Working within a large team taught me how important visual consistency and communication are when several artists contribute to the same environment.
  • I improved my ability to balance visual fidelity, optimization and real-time constraints, particularly through the production of Celaena's three pairs of wings.
  • Working part-time on the project required me to prioritize tasks efficiently and work independently within a very limited production time.

What I Would Improve

  • I would dedicate more time to retopology and optimization, especially around complex deformation areas such as the wings.
  • I would establish clearer material and texture guidelines earlier in production to make the environment's visual cohesion easier to maintain across the team.
  • I would plan the asset pipeline earlier to leave more time for polish and iteration before the final integration.
