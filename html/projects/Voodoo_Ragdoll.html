Concept to Final Design

Voodoo Ragdoll is an original character concept created for Whispers of the Dollhouse, a local couch-multiplayer game developed as a school project at IIM Paris. Several iterations were explored before settling on this creepy-cute rag doll, with button eyes, stitched fabric skin and a torn plaid dress.

High Poly Sculpt

Full turnaround of the high poly sculpt, modeled in ZBrush. The knitted hair, fabric folds and stitching details were sculpted to read clearly as a handmade textile doll.

Texturing

Final PBR texturing created in Substance 3D Painter, focusing on woven fabric, visible stitching and slightly worn materials to sell the handmade doll feel.

Retopology & Optimization

High poly, low poly and wireframe comparison. The mesh was retopologized and optimized for real-time use, keeping the silhouette and proportions readable in-game.

In-Game Integration

Voodoo Ragdoll was integrated into Whispers of the Dollhouse using Unity, playable in local split-screen multiplayer.
