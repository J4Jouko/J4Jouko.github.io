Enthaur: Lost in Slumber is a first-person adventure game developed at IIM Paris, in which the player explores the decaying, living interior of a dying homunculus. I was part of the Game Art team, working on character concept and creature design, several key 3D props, and early-game level art.

Game Overview

A strange world to discover... You are a homunculus, a being created out of despair. Your world is a vast, living creature, now dying, too weak to create another like you.

As the last of your kind, your survival is bound to its recovery. Heal the organism, or perish with it. Guided by the remnants and tools left behind by previous homunculi, you must act quickly, as something else has already claimed its place inside.

Discover a decaying world inside what seems to be a dying homunculus, use what you will find in this soon-to-be rotten creature, and try to save it... or at least save yourself...

Gameplay

Move in first person through the creature's interior, climbing flesh, crossing cavities, and pulling yourself through collapsing passages.

Use your lantern to reveal paths, detect parasites, and survive areas swallowed by darkness.

Manipulate sinews, tissues, and organic mechanisms to open routes, stabilize zones, and solve environmental puzzles.

Avoid parasites through stealth and timing, hiding, fleeing, and using the environment to stay unseen.

Heal failing areas of the creature to keep it alive... and keep yourself alive with it.

The Parasite

The parasite was fully designed and modeled by me, from early 2D exploration to the final textured 3D creature.

Research moodboard, exploratory 2D sketches, and the final concept with a full color turnaround.

Low poly 3D model, sculpted in ZBrush and built in Blender, shown in wireframe from front, side, top and bottom to check it stayed faithful to the concept.

The parasite was planned to be animated in-game, so the model was carefully split into three separate parts to make the animator's work easier. Reinforcements were added around future fold and bend areas (legs, mouth and body) to support the range of motion needed for animation.

Final textured version of the parasite, with textures created in Substance 3D Painter.

Parasite In-Game

After I left the team, the parasite was rigged and animated by Reda Lotfi during the second production phase. The model's part separation and reinforced fold areas were built specifically to support this animation work.

Character Hands

I modeled the hands of the character the player embodies. High poly sculpted in ZBrush, retopologized in Blender, and textured in Substance 3D Painter.

Lungs & Heart

I designed and modeled the lungs prop, a key organic landmark inside the creature's body.

Moodboard and inspiration references, exploratory concepts, then the 3D model sculpted in ZBrush and textured in Substance 3D Painter.

I also modeled, textured and integrated a heart, plugged directly into the lungs.

Amber Source

I modeled and textured the amber source prop, based on a concept created by Résa Lotfi. Two versions were made: one with a bone-like base for platforms, and one with a muscle-like base for organic surfaces.

Level Art

Project History

Enthaur started as a first production phase during my M1 year, with a 5-month development period, during which I worked part-time alongside a work-study contract.

I then left the team to start Deilist: And Nothing Remains, my end-of-studies M2 game project. Enthaur continued to evolve without me during the following two months of M2, with the parasite being rigged and animated by Reda Lotfi.
